Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

Speech Tracker is a web application designed to help users record and review speech therapy session progress. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use Speech Tracker.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Speech Tracker, we may collect the following information:

  • Email address used for login or account creation.
  • Authentication information handled through Supabase and/or Google login.
  • Client names entered by the user.
  • Speech session data, such as target sound, level, cues, correct attempts, incorrect attempts, accuracy, and session date.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Allow you to save and review speech therapy progress records.
  • Display client-specific session history and progress data.
  • Provide login options such as email login, one-time code login, and Google login.

3. Google Login

If you choose to log in with Google, Speech Tracker may receive basic profile information from Google, such as your email address, for authentication purposes only.

We do not sell your Google account information or use it for advertising.

4. Data Storage

Speech Tracker uses Supabase to provide authentication and database storage. Your account and session data may be stored securely through Supabase services.

5. Client and Session Data

You are responsible for the information you enter into Speech Tracker. Avoid entering unnecessary sensitive personal information. Client names and session data are used only to help you organize and track therapy progress.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data only when required to operate the application, comply with the law, or protect the security of the service.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. However, no online service can guarantee complete security. You should keep your login details safe and avoid sharing your account with others.

8. Your Choices

You may stop using Speech Tracker at any time. If you want your account or stored data deleted, please contact us using the email below.

9. Children's Privacy

Speech Tracker is intended for use by adults, educators, therapists, or caregivers. If the app is used to record information about children, the user is responsible for obtaining any required consent.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
